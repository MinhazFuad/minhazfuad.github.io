About Me

I'm a final-year B.Sc. CS&E student at United International University, Dhaka, majoring in Data Science. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, NLP, and full-stack engineering — I build things that work in the real world, not just in notebooks.

I'm an IEEE-published researcher (ICCIT 2025), an arXiv preprint author, and currently completing my undergraduate thesis on a waste-detection VLM dataset called WADE — which achieved a 9.4× recall improvement and reduced hallucinations from 0.683 to 0.088, surpassing commercial systems.

I build production platforms with Next.js, Supabase, and PostgreSQL and prototype embedded systems with Raspberry Pi 5 and OpenCV. Outside code, I was Campus Champion at Hult Prize 2026 and have interned as a graphic designer.
