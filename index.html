About

Research depth, product speed.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from United International University, majoring in Data Science. My work sits at the intersection of NLP, LLM, and multimodal AI research and full-stack web engineering.

I co-author research on efficient LLM inference, waste-vision datasets, and lightweight detection — and I build the products around that research, from a computer-vision annotation platform to a learn-to-earn marketplace. I care about work that moves from an idea to something people can actually use.
